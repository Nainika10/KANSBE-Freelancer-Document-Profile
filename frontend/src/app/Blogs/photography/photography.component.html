What to Look for
in a Lensman

How to
Choose

Clear Thinking

An artist must be able to articulate 'why' they make a choice. If they cannot explain their lighting, they cannot replicate success.

Consistency

One great photo is luck. A portfolio of 50 great photos is a signature. Look for the thread that connects their work.

Communication

The best technical photographer fails if they cannot command a room or listen to a client's nuance. Communication is the catalyst.

Portfolio Depth

Don't just look at their best shots; look for consistency across whole albums.

Style Alignment

Do they prefer moody shadows or bright, clinical high-key lighting?

Post-Production

Understand the editing turnaround and the level of retouching provided.

Chemical Bond

The relationship between the subject and shooter is visible in the frame.
